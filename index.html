A game about being in the zone

flOw was Jenova Chen's thesis game at the University of Southern California's Interactive Media program, built with Nicholas Clark and scored by Austin Wintory. It was one of the games built for his thesis, Flow in Games: the idea that a game should keep each player in Csikszentmihalyi's flow zone, between boredom and anxiety, and that the player should be the one steering the difficulty.

So there is no menu and no difficulty setting. You are a small creature in a blue sea. Eat what's around you and you grow. Eat the red food when you're ready for something harder and you sink a level, where the water gets darker and the other creatures get bigger. Eat the blue food when it's too much and you float back up. Every player tunes the game to themselves without being asked to.

It was shown at the Experimental Gameplay Workshop at GDC 2006, and people played it a lot. "Addicting" was the word Jenova's fans used most. A year later the same idea, rebuilt by his new studio thatgamecompany, launched on the PlayStation 3. That studio went on to make Flower and Journey, Austin Wintory composed the music for Journey, and the blue sea on this page is where all that started.

You can read Jenova Chen's thesis, Flow in Games, on his site.

Why this launcher

  • The copies on archive sites like archive.org don't work. They host the game's SWF on its own, but the game loads its levels (levels.xml) and its music (41 MP3s) from files that sit beside it, and those aren't there. The game boots into a black sea with nothing to eat.
  • Jenova Chen's own flOw page still has a link to play the game, but it leads to a site which can't be reached.
  • Every file the game needs is still on Jenova Chen's site. This page puts them back together, unmodified, so the game is playable again.

Make your own levels

Everything about a level lives in levels.xml: its colour, its size and what swims in it. Start from Jenova Chen's levels, change what you like, and play it above. Your level sets are saved in this browser only.

    What the game understands
    <Campaign num="1" player="Snakefish">
    The game starts at campaign 1, which must come first. The last level's first creature carries a gold egg; eat it and you float to the top and hatch as the next campaign's player (Snakefish or Jellyfish).
    <Level bgColor="0x00BFFF" levelSize="400">
    One level, in order from the surface down. The first level of campaign 1 is the title screen. The game has music for 20 levels; deeper ones are silent. Red food is added to every level but the last, and blue to every level but the first.
    <SpawnFood num foodType hpMin hpVar>
    foodType 0 to 5 picks the kind; hpMin plus up to hpVar sets its size.
    <SpawnFish>, <SpawnJellyfish>, <SpawnFlockfish>
    num numSegs maxSegs randEvolve segLength speedMin speedVar turnMin turnVar, and panic="true" to make them flee.
    <SpawnManta>, <SpawnBoss>
    num numSegs segLength speedMin speedVar turnMin turnVar.
    <SpawnBillboard name posX posY>
    Text in the water: flowing_title, credits, tobecontinued or warning.
